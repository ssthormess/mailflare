## Product grounding

Read `README.md`, `mailflare-landing/app/page.tsx`, `docs/api.md`, and `src/components/agent/send-review.tsx`, and viewed the existing inbox screenshot and app icon before writing the copy. The UI in the film is an animated product illustration with fictional sample mail. The human confirmation step reflects the implementation's review-before-send flow.

## Render

Run `python3 render.py` from this directory. Requires Pillow and FFmpeg. Functions are in `utils.py`. No application files or previous video assets are modified.
